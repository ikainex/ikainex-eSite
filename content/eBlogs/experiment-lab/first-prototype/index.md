## Start with a measurable question

A good experiment begins with a question that can be tested. Document the expected behavior, test setup, instruments, and conditions.

## Record what actually happened

Include measurements, observations, unexpected behavior, and changes made during the experiment.

## The takeaway

The goal of an experiment log is not to make every prototype look successful. It is to preserve useful engineering knowledge that can inform the next iteration.
