## Learning path

1. **Set up the ADC.** Check the board’s pinout and reference voltage before connecting it to your Nucleo.
2. **Run the monitor.** Build and flash the STM32CubeIDE example, then open the serial terminal.
3. **Verify a measurement.** Compare the displayed voltage with a known input and a multimeter reading.
